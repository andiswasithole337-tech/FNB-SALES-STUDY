# FNB-SALES-STUDY
#OVERVIEW
This project is a data analysis case study completed using a simulated retail sales dataset provided by FNB. The dataset contains daily sales information for a single product sold at a large retail store, including the sales value, cost of sales, quantity sold, and transaction date.
The objective of the case study was to analyse the sales performance of the product, calculate key business metrics, evaluate profitability, identify promotional periods, and generate meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

#OBJECTIVE
The analysis focused on answering the following business questions:
Calculate the daily sales price per unit.
Determine the average unit sales price.
Calculate the daily gross profit percentage.
Calculate the daily gross profit per unit.
Identify promotional periods and evaluate the product's price elasticity of demand.
Generate additional insights using KPIs, reports, and dashboard visualisations.

Key Insights
The analysis provided several valuable business insights, including:
-Daily selling prices varied throughout the reporting period, indicating possible pricing adjustments.
-Gross profit percentages identified both profitable and loss-making trading days.
-Promotion periods were identified by comparing the daily selling price with the overall average selling price.
-Gross profit per unit provided a clearer understanding of product profitability at an individual unit level.
-KPI analysis highlighted the overall financial performance of the product across the reporting period.

These insights can assist business stakeholders in evaluating pricing strategies, promotional effectiveness, and profitability.

#TOOLS USED
-Databricks SQL-cleaning data
-Microsoft Excel-analysis on excel
-Miro-for planning the project
-Power Bi and Data Studio- for data visualization/dashboards

#SUMMARY
This case study demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow, from understanding and preparing raw data to developing business metrics and extracting actionable insights. By calculating profitability, analysing pricing behaviour, and identifying promotional trends, the project showcases how SQL can be used to transform raw sales data into meaningful information that supports strategic business decisions.
